Getting help

Contact the Bergstrom IT department: rkraft@bergstromelectric.com · (701) 775-8897.

Common questions

  • "Not connected" / can't sign in — the app reaches the company server on the office network or the company VPN. Connect the VPN and try again. Everything you enter while offline is kept on the phone and sent when you're connected.
  • Which login? — the same username and password as the Bergstrom company apps (the launcher).
  • A job is missing — your list shows the jobs you're assigned to as foreman, project manager or branch manager. Ask the office if a job should be added.
  • Photos — allow camera access when asked to add photos to an inspection (Settings → BEI Field → Camera).
