BEI Field — Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

BEI Field is a private app made for employees of Bergstrom Electric, Inc. ("Bergstrom", "we"). It is distributed only to Bergstrom through Apple Business and requires a Bergstrom company login. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

What the app uses

  • Your company login (username and password). It is used only to sign in to Bergstrom's own company server and is stored securely in the iPhone/iPad Keychain so the app can sign you in again.
  • Work information you enter: job progress (% complete by phase), equipment sign-out, return and reservations, vehicle inspections (including odometer readings, notes and photos you take or choose), and panel schedules.
  • Job information from Bergstrom: the jobs, phases, equipment, trucks and (for project managers) job-cost figures you are allowed to see.
  • Camera and photo library: only when you choose to add a photo to an inspection.

Where it goes

Information you enter is sent only to Bergstrom's own company server, over the company network or the company VPN, and is used to run Bergstrom's business (progress reporting, equipment and fleet management, inspections, panel schedules). The app keeps an offline copy on your device so it works without a connection; that copy is removed when you sign out.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, does not collect your location, and does not sell or share your information with anyone outside Bergstrom, except as required by law.

Keeping and deleting information

Work records (such as inspections and progress reports) are kept by Bergstrom as business records. To ask about, correct or remove information, contact the Bergstrom IT department below.

Children

BEI Field is for Bergstrom employees only and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
