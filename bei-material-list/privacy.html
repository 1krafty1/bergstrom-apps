BEI Material List — Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

BEI Material List is a private app made for employees of Bergstrom Electric, Inc. ("Bergstrom", "we"). Crews use it to build a parts list for a job and share it with the shop. It is distributed only to Bergstrom through Apple Business. It has no sign-in.

What stays on your device

Your material lists (job name, date needed, delivery or pick-up, notes, parts and quantities) and the name you type in Settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. We do not receive them.

What leaves your device

  • Lists you share. When you tap Share PDF (or CSV), the iOS share sheet sends the file where you choose (for example Mail, Messages or AirDrop). The app does not send it anywhere by itself.
  • The parts list. When it has a signal the app downloads the current Bergstrom parts list (a public file). No information about you is sent with that download.
  • Office publishing (office staff only). Office staff with the office PIN can publish a new parts list from the app. That sends the PIN, the new parts list and the publisher's name (shown as "Parts list updated … by …") to Bergstrom's parts-list service. Crews who don't publish never send this.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, does not collect your location, and does not sell or share your information.

Deleting information

Delete a list in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it saved. Forget PIN in Settings removes the office PIN from the device.

Children

BEI Material List is for Bergstrom employees only and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
