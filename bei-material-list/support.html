Getting help

Contact the Bergstrom IT department: rkraft@bergstromelectric.com · (701) 775-8897.

Common questions

  • No signal — the app works with no signal. The parts list is on the phone; it updates by itself the next time you have a connection.
  • A part isn't in the list — use "Add item not in the list" and type it in. Ask the office to add it to the parts list.
  • Sending a list — open the list and tap Share PDF, then pick Mail, Messages, AirDrop or another app. "Share as spreadsheet (CSV)" sends it as a spreadsheet instead.
  • Same list for another job — open the list and tap Copy to a new job.
  • Updating the parts list (office) — Settings → Update parts list (office). It needs the office PIN from IT.
