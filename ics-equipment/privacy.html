ICS Equipment — Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

ICS Equipment is a private iPad and iPhone app made for employees of Industrial Contract Services, Inc. ("ICS", "we") to track the company's tools and equipment: where each item is, who has it, its photos, servicing and inspections, equipment requests and rentals. It is distributed privately through Apple Business, is not offered to the public, and requires an ICS company login. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

What the app uses

  • Your company login (username or email, and password). It is sent only to ICS's own equipment server to sign you in. The app does not store your password; the server's sign-in session keeps you signed in for up to 12 hours of inactivity.
  • Your account details from ICS: your name, email address and role, so the app shows what your role may do and records who made each change.
  • What you enter: moves and check-ins, item details, service and inspection records, comments, equipment requests, rental records, and photos of equipment you take or choose. Each is saved on ICS's server with your name, as the company's equipment record.
  • Camera: to scan equipment tags (the scanning happens on the device) and, when you choose, to photograph equipment. Photo library: only when you choose an existing photo of a piece of equipment.
  • Local network: the app talks directly to ICS's equipment server on the ICS office and yard Wi-Fi.

Where it goes

Everything is sent only to ICS's own equipment server, which is reachable only on the ICS company network. The app keeps only its settings (the server address) and a cache of equipment photos on the device.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, does not collect your location, and does not sell or share your information with anyone outside ICS, except as required by law.

Keeping and deleting information

Equipment records, including who moved or changed an item, are kept by ICS as company records. To ask about, correct or remove information, contact us below.

Children

ICS Equipment is for ICS employees only and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
