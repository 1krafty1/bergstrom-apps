Common questions

  • "Not connected" / can't sign in — the app reaches the company server on the office network or the company VPN. Turn on the VPN and try again. An inspection you fill out offline waits in the Outbox until you tap Send.
  • Which login? — the same username and password as the Bergstrom company apps (the launcher).
  • A truck is missing — ask the office to add it to the fleet list, then open the app again with the VPN on.
  • Photos — allow camera access when asked (Settings → BEI Inspect → Camera).
