BEI Inspect — Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

BEI Inspect is a private app made for employees of Bergstrom Electric, Inc. ("Bergstrom", "we") to do vehicle inspections of company trucks. It is distributed only to Bergstrom through Apple Business and requires a Bergstrom company login. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

What the app uses

  • Your company login (username and password). It is used only to sign in to Bergstrom's own company server and is stored securely in the iPhone/iPad Keychain so the app can sign you in again.
  • Inspection information you enter: the truck, date, odometer and oil-change readings, checklist answers, notes and comments, and photos you take or choose.
  • Information from Bergstrom: the list of company trucks, their managers and your past inspections.
  • Camera and photo library: only when you choose to add a photo to an inspection.

Where it goes

Inspections are sent only to Bergstrom's own company server, over the company network or the company VPN, and the report is emailed to the truck's manager. The app keeps drafts and an offline copy of the lists on your device so it works without a connection; the saved copy is removed when you sign out.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, does not collect your location, and does not sell or share your information with anyone outside Bergstrom, except as required by law.

Keeping and deleting information

Inspections are kept by Bergstrom as fleet records. To ask about, correct or remove information, contact the Bergstrom IT department below.

Children

BEI Inspect is for Bergstrom employees only and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
